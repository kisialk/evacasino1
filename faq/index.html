RTP & max win

What is the RTP?

TaDa states 94.38%. Many third parties still quote 97%. The JILI card we checked shows no RTP. Full breakdown: RTP & volatility.

What is the max win?

Lead with 10,000× on JILI and TaDa product cards. TaDa body copy also cites 15,025×. We publish both with attribution.

Is Extra Bet “better RTP”?

TaDa describes Extra Bet as a cost and reel/wheel modifier (+50% stake). It does not publish a claim that Extra Bet raises RTP by 50%. Treat it as price, not edge.
