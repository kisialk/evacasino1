Setting a stake

Lines are fixed at five — that part isn't a decision. What you choose is total stake per spin, sized so you can repeat it through a quiet stretch without flinching. TaDa's page cites a wide window (0.2 to 200); other listings show different mapped ranges entirely. Always check your own client — the bet range is a currency-mapping detail, not a locked spec.

One paid spin, three steps

1. Evaluation

Reels stop. The grid pays left to right across five lines — no ways-style logic, no cluster pays. If you're unsure how ties resolve, the client's rules panel has the answer; open it once.

2. Special-reel multiply

On a paying line, the win gets multiplied by whatever's sitting in the special reel's center slot. TaDa lists 1×–15× as the possible values.

3. Settle or wheel

If the center shows the wheel symbol instead of a multiplier, the round can move to the Lucky Wheel. Otherwise the stake resolves and the board goes quiet.

Say those three steps out loud a few times during demo play. Skip step two and you'll misremember why a "small" gem hit paid well, or why a pretty board barely paid anything.

Garuda while you're learning

Garuda substitutes for the regular symbols and pays 3× bet for three-of-a-kind on its own — the top line pay before any special-reel multiplier applies. It isn't a free-spins scatter. Full ladder: symbols.

Optional Extra Bet on the same spin

Switch it on before you spin if your plan can absorb the +50% stake uplift. TaDa states 1× leaves the special reel and the wheel path improves. Switch it off when you're just practising line reading.

When the Lucky Wheel opens

Treat it as a separate prize draw with a published ladder — not a free-spins meter. Full guide: Lucky Wheel.

Controls worth knowing

Auto Spin and turbo speed exist in most skins. Use the stop conditions your client offers as actual brakes. Turbo changes animation pace, not the math underneath it — skipping the spin animation doesn't change what already landed.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting around for free spins that aren't in this build.
  • Ignoring the special reel because a gem line looked pretty.
  • Leaving Extra Bet on because the last wheel spin was good.
  • Reading coin values off a screenshot taken at a different stake.
  • Assuming Turbo changes the odds instead of just the pace.

Practice order that actually sticks

Line evaluation first. Special-reel reading second. Extra Bet third. Lucky Wheel last. Reversing that order — hunting the wheel on day one — is how people skip the fundamentals and go straight to chasing. The demo guide runs this exact order on a timer.
