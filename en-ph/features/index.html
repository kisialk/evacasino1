The special multiplier reel

On every spin, the fourth reel shows a value from the set TaDa lists — 1×–15× — with 1× staying in the mix when Extra Bet is off. A paying line on the 3×3 gets multiplied by whatever's sitting in the center slot. That's why this game feels "always on" compared with slots that make you wait for a bonus trigger to matter.

Extra Bet — what you're actually buying

TaDa states Extra Bet costs an additional 50% of stake. What changes:

  • 1× removed from the special reel
  • Lucky Wheel path upgraded
  • Wheel wins can pick up an extra random multiplier from the 1×–15× set

That's a volatility-and-cost trade you choose per spin or per session — not a loyalty perk. Strategy framing: strategy notes.

Quick check: if you can't say what Extra Bet actually removes from the reel, you're not ready to leave it on for a full Auto Spin batch. Learn the base multiplier set first.

Lucky Wheel lives next door

Full mechanics are on its own page. What matters here: the trigger sits on the special-reel center, and Extra Bet changes how good that center's upgrade path is.

What Fortune Gems 2 features are not

No free-spins round. No feature buy into free spins. No progressive jackpot on the cards we've checked. If an article pasted those sections onto this page, it copied a different game.

Also missing: any "meter that carries across spins" layer. Each stake resolves on its own — whatever the special reel shows that spin, or a wheel detour. That simplicity is the appeal, and also the trap, because the pace makes Extra Bet easy to leave switched on without noticing.

How the pieces actually interact

A base line hit with a weak multiplier is filler. The same hit under 10×–15× is why people remember this game. A wheel symbol swaps the multiplier story for a ladder draw entirely. Extra Bet edits both stories at once. Practising only one of those modes means you don't know the game yet — run the demo checklist across all three.

On mobile

The compact 3×3 plus special reel reads fine in portrait as long as the skin keeps the fourth reel visible. If a skin buries Extra Bet inside a menu, open it once before you Auto Spin.

Thumb reach is the real risk: people flip Extra Bet on by accident more often on mobile than on desktop. Check your total stake after any settings change.
