Garuda wild

Garuda lands on the main 3×3 and substitutes for the other regular symbols (TaDa). Three Garuda on a line is also the highest listed line pay — 3× bet — before any special-reel multiplier applies.

It is not a free-spins scatter. It does not open the Lucky Wheel by itself. Wheel triggers sit on the special reel's center slot; see Lucky Wheel.

How a line actually pays

Example using TaDa's rule set: three red gems pay 2.40× bet. If the special-reel center shows 5×, that line becomes 12× bet for the spin (before any other lines add). Same gems under 1× stay at 2.40×. That is why watching the fourth reel matters as much as recognising the gem.

Extra Bet does not rewrite these base pays; it changes which multipliers sit on the special reel and how the wheel path behaves. Details: features.

Screenshot traps

Coin displays scale with stake. A paytable screenshot from someone else's bet level will look “wrong” next to yours even when the ratios match. Open the in-client paytable at your stake, then check that the ordering matches TaDa's ladder above.

Operator skins sometimes rename buttons or restyle symbols. If the live help text conflicts with this page, trust the client for that wallet and email us a correction with a screenshot — contact.
