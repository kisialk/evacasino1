Bankroll blocks

Split the cash you are willing to lose into session blocks sized for medium-volatility quiet stretches. When a block is gone, the session ends. Borrowing from tomorrow because the wheel “felt close” is how blocks become one continuous chase.

Stake sizing

Pick a stake you can repeat for a full block without needing a “comeback” spin. Lines are fixed at five, so the only lever is total stake (and Extra Bet). Confirm live bet windows in your client — TaDa cites 0.2–200; other listings differ. PHP amounts are operator mappings, not a second math model.

Extra Bet rules (write these down)

Extra Bet costs +50% stake (TaDa). Decide before launch:

  • Off for learning sessions and for any block where cost certainty matters more than wheel upgrades.
  • On only inside a capped spin count (example: 20 Extra Bet spins, then forced off).
  • Never enable Extra Bet mid-block solely because the last wheel was small.

What you buy mechanically: features.

Wheel chasing

Lucky Wheel clips are memorable; dry hours are more common. Raising stake after a small wheel stop, or after no wheel at all, is chase behaviour. Cap “wheel hunt” spins the same way you cap Extra Bet. Mechanics: Lucky Wheel. Numbers: RTP.

Auto Spin

If you Auto Spin, set loss and single-win stops the client allows. Skipping animation while Extra Bet is on hides how fast the block burns. Turbo changes pace, not math.

Hard stops

  • Time stop
  • Loss stop (end of block)
  • Win stop (walk away — write the number first)
  • Extra Bet spin budget
