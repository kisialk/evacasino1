The RTP conflict — what we publish

TaDa Gaming's English product page states an RTP of 94.38%, backed by a large simulation claim. JILI Games's product card — the one we checked — shows no RTP figure at all. Most third-party reviews, plus the old fortunegems-2.com archive, still quote 97%.

We won't average those into a comfortable "official 97%." When 97% appears here, it's labelled third-party. When 94.38% appears, it's named as TaDa's page. Source list: Sources.

Max win — card figure versus TaDa's body copy

Both JILI and TaDa product cards show 10,000×. TaDa's long-form text also repeats 15,025× a few paragraphs down. We lead with the card figure and mention 15,025× only as a separate TaDa-body claim. Neither number promises your session gets anywhere near it.

Volatility on the cards

Both provider cards tag this Medium (MED). Medium means quiet stretches and spiky wheel moments both happen. It doesn't mean gentle, and it doesn't mean safe.

How to actually use these numbers

RTP is a long-sample average — useful for sizing expectations over thousands of spins, useless for predicting the next ten minutes. Max-win language describes the ceiling printed on the product page; it doesn't justify chasing a stake toward it. Extra Bet changes your cost per spin. It doesn't remove your need for a stop.

If your own operator's help screen shows a different RTP than TaDa's page, believe the client you're actually playing on — our citations describe the published spec, not every regional build. Ask the operator which configuration you're running before arguing with a screenshot from another site.

What "medium" feels like without a hit-rate sheet

Provider cards tag MED volatility without publishing a bonus-frequency percentage anywhere on the same page. In practice: expect enough small line activity to stay engaged, and expect wheel gaps that run longer than any highlight reel suggests. Neither pattern proves which RTP figure is correct.

Bet windows — a config detail, not gospel

TaDa cites 0.2–200. Other listings show 0.10–100, sometimes 150 with Extra Bet active. Treat these ranges as operator- and currency-dependent. Confirm in your own client — a PHP display is a currency mapping, not a second math model, unless the operator says otherwise.

Myths this page exists to kill

  • "97% must be official — an aggregator said so."
  • "15,025× quietly replaced 10,000×."
  • "A dry hour on the wheel means the RTP is broken."
  • "Extra Bet raises RTP by 50%." It raises stake cost. TaDa never claims an RTP bump.
