Fortune Gems vs Fortune Gems 2

What actually changes between the original Fortune Gems and Fortune Gems 2.

By Fortune Gems Desk · Updated 31 Jul 2026 · How we review

Both games share a compact grid and a multiplier lane. Fortune Gems 2's real pitch is the Lucky Wheel and Extra Bet — not free spins. Neither title has a free-spins round in the feature set we publish.

What carries over

Small board. Five-line left-to-right thinking. Gem / temple dressing. If you already know how a multiplier lane next to a tiny grid feels, FG2 will not feel foreign for the first ten spins.

What Fortune Gems 2 changes

  • Lucky Wheel — wheel symbol in the special-reel center opens a prize ladder (up to 1,000× listed on TaDa). Full page: Lucky Wheel.
  • Extra Bet — +50% stake (TaDa); removes 1× from the special reel; upgrades the wheel path. Features.
  • Ceiling language on FG2 cards — lead max win 10,000×, volatility Medium (MED). Do not paste those onto FG1 without FG1's own sources.

Which to open
